## Description
Put your hand on the side of a running fridge and it's often warm. That's not a fault. A refrigerator doesn't make cold; it moves heat, pumping it out of your food and into your kitchen through a loop of boiling and squeezing. Then there is the part you never see: a second heater, mounted right next to the coldest coil.
